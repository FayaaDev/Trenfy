# Requirements Baseline - v1.3 (Archived)

This archive captures the final v1.3 scope after category-grid, bookmarks, and profile-delivery phases were removed before ship. The shipped milestone focused on the branded mobile feed foundation, filters, and theming; removed items are recorded as dropped from v1.3 scope rather than accepted gaps.

## Outcome Definitions

- `validated` - shipped as intended and supported by phase summary or shipped code.
- `adjusted` - shipped with a documented wording change or scope refinement while still meeting the milestone goal.
- `dropped` - removed from the final v1.3 milestone scope.

- `NAV-02` adjusted: v1.3 shipped the nested category stack and `CategoryFeed` route wiring, but the richer category-card drill-down experience moved out when later mobile phases were removed from the milestone.
